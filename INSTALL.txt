EDRIS EDUCATION — PDF-REFERENCE UPDATE
======================================

This update follows the supplied "UI UX Education Edris.pdf" visually by using
high-quality renders of the actual approved PDF pages as the education UI.

WHY THIS METHOD
---------------
The PDF uses many exact photos, Arabic text, bilingual Malay/English copy,
card positions, shadows and typography. Rendering the approved pages directly
keeps those details visually faithful instead of approximating them in CSS.
